COLLECTOR CURRENT (IC) • PS-37
$$I_C = \min(\beta \times I_B,\; I_{C(sat)})$$
In saturation, IC(sat) = (12V − 0.2V) / 120Ω = 98.33 mA (≈ 100 mA), driving the rated doorbell buzzer. In the active region, IC = β × IB (β = 80).

POWER DISSIPATION (PD)
$$P_D = V_{CE} \times I_C$$
The transistor dissipates power as heat, equal to the product of the collector-emitter voltage and collector current. The TO-92 package (2N2222) is rated for a maximum of 625 mW. Exceeding this limit can cause thermal damage.

ENGINEERING NOTE: BASE DRIVE AT 2 mA LIMIT VS. DEEP SATURATION

Theoretical Minimum: RB = 1.3 kΩ is a theoretical minimum calculated under assumed 0.7 V VBE and 3.3 V loaded sensor input (IB = 2.0 mA, βforced = 50).
Overdrive Clarification: The nominal 1.6× overdrive factor compares available base drive with active-region estimate (100 mA / 80 = 1.25 mA). An increase in base drive is not proof of deep saturation.
Datasheet Warning: Guaranteeing VCE(sat) ≤ 0.2 V in a 2N2222 typically requires βforced ≤ 10; achieving VCE(sat) ≤ 0.2 V at βforced = 50 still requires datasheet confirmation or bench measurement.
