ABOUT BJT LABX

Understanding BJT Technology Through Interactive Learning and Simulation

Project Summary • PS-37

BJT LABX (PS-37) is an interactive educational platform designed for the comprehensive analysis and simulation of a BJT Current Amplifier Switch for High-Decibel Doorbell Buzzers. The system interfaces a weak sensor signal (2 mA at 3.3 V) with a high-current doorbell buzzer drawing 100 mA at 12 V (load resistance RL = 120 Ω) using an NPN transistor (2N2222, minimum gain β = 80) operating in saturation and cut-off modes. The platform calculates optimal base resistance (RB(design) = 1.3 kΩ for deep saturation with an overdrive factor of 1.6×, and RB(threshold) ≈ 2.11 kΩ for buzzer switching), evaluates transistor power dissipation to guarantee thermal safety, and demonstrates inductive kickback suppression using a 1N4001 flyback clamp diode.
